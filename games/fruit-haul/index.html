Fruit Haul: Truck Runner

Drive, dodge & collect fruits in this fun seasonal truck runner. Chain combos, climb the leaderboards, and unlock achievements.

About the game

Hop in the truck and hit the road for a fast, friendly arcade run. Swerve around obstacles, scoop up fruit, and keep your combo alive as the seasons change around you. Easy to pick up, satisfying to master — perfect for a quick break or a high-score chase.

Features

  • Seasonal themes & dynamic weather effects
  • Combo & mega-combo scoring system
  • Score milestones and reward bursts
  • Google Play leaderboards & achievements
  • Daily challenges to keep things fresh
  • Smart in-game notifications
