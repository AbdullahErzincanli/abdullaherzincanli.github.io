My Idle Harvest

Grow, manage, and automate your dream farm in this cozy idle farming simulator. Hire helpful workers, build processing facilities, and reset for stars to multiply your profits.

▶ Get it onGoogle Play

About the game

Step into the boots of a farm owner and turn a modest plot of land into a thriving agricultural empire. Plant and harvest fresh crops, raise farm animals, and build production facilities to turn raw materials into high-value products. With offline progress, your farm keeps growing even when you're away.

Features

  • Automate planting and harvesting with Scarecrows, Farmers, and Tractors
  • Boost productivity with advanced systems like Sprinklers and Drones
  • Raise animals (Chickens, Cows) to produce Eggs and Milk
  • Establish production facilities (Flour Mill, Bakery, Jam Maker) for complex supply chains
  • Complete orders for local merchants to maximize gold earnings
  • Prestige system: Reset your farm to gain Stars and permanent profit multipliers
  • Dynamic rewarded boosts for double production, animal yields, and auto-fulfillment
  • Play offline and claim offline earnings when you return
