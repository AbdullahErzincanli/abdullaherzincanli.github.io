Dicebound

Roll your own destiny in this tabletop-inspired D&D adventure game. Step into a world ruled by the Ninefold cosmology, forge your cracked Fate Die into a legendary d20, and write your own story.

About the game

In a world where every life is governed by a cosmic roll of the Ninefold dice, you are born in a null roll. Lacking a pre-determined fate, you are a ghost in the administration of the Order of the Fixed Face. But your absence of destiny is your greatest strength: you can roll again. Journey across a fractured world to collect Face-Shards, upgrade your Fate Die, build bonds with companion travelers, and choose whether to halt the cosmic machine or claim true free will.

Features

  • Tabletop-inspired RPG adventure with rich, text-driven branching narratives
  • Dice cosmology mechanics: upgrade your Fate Die (d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → d20)
  • Tactical choices and combat driven by authentic tabletop-style rolls
  • Explore unique regions such as Highcandle, Ember Marches, Coyn, and Saltbreak
  • Interact with companion characters, develop deep bonds, and unlock their personal quests
  • 100% offline gameplay preserving battery and your personal data
  • Completely ad-free and tracking-free experience
