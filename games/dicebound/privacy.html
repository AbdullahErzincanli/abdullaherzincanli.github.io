Privacy Policy for Dicebound

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AbnaSoftware ("we", "us", or "the developer") handles information in connection with our mobile game, Dicebound (the "Game"). By downloading or using the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

Summary

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or other personal details.
  • Your game progress, campaign state, companions, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • The Game operates entirely offline and does not use third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertisements.

Information collected

1. Data stored on your device

Game data such as your current level, unlocked companion bonds, campaign progression, Fate Die upgrades, inventory items, and options (like sound and text display settings) is stored locally on your device using Flutter's local storage capabilities. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us. Removing the Game from your device deletes this data.

2. No External Server Transmission

Dicebound is designed as a standalone offline experience. It does not connect to external servers to upload gameplay data, nor does it track your behaviors or usage across other apps or websites.

Third-party services

Unlike some of our other games, Dicebound does not utilize advertising networks (like Google AdMob) or analytics suites (like Firebase Analytics). It is a fully self-contained, ad-free and tracking-free experience.

Children's privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

Data retention & security

Data stored on your device remains until you delete the Game or clear its data. We rely on the native security measures of your device's operating system to protect the locally stored application data.

Your rights

You have full control over the data stored by the Game. For data held on your device, you can exercise your rights directly by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling it from your device. You may also contact us with any questions or requests.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
